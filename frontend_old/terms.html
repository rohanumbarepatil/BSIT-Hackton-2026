Information We Collect

When you use WasteWatchers, we collect the following information:

  • Account data — username, email address, college name
  • Usage data — waste items scanned, eco-points earned, games played, rewards redeemed
  • Images — photos you upload for waste classification (processed and not permanently stored)
  • Location data — only if you use the "Locate Me" feature on the Bin Map, and only with your explicit permission
  • Device data — browser type, device type, used to improve compatibility

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve WasteWatchers:

  • To authenticate your account and manage your eco-points balance
  • To classify waste items using our AI model
  • To display leaderboard rankings within the campus community
  • To process reward redemptions
  • To improve platform features and fix issues

Images & Camera Access

When you scan a waste item, your image is sent to our AI classification service for processing. Images are not permanently stored on our servers after classification is complete. Camera access is requested only when you tap "Take Photo" and is never accessed in the background.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Data may be shared only in the following limited cases:

  • With Kalinga University administration for aggregate campus sustainability reporting (no personal identifiers)
  • With reward partners (e.g. canteen) only to validate a redeemed voucher code
  • When required by law or university policy

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, all personal data is removed within 30 days. Aggregate anonymised usage data (e.g. total scans per category) may be retained for research purposes.

Cookies & Local Storage

WasteWatchers uses browser local storage to remember your daily game progress and session state. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. No tracking pixels or analytics services are currently active on this platform.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time via browser settings

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and hashed password storage. However, no system is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password for your account.
