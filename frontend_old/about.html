How It Works
Three steps to a cleaner campus
01
Snap Waste
Point your camera at any waste item. Our AI classifies it instantly — plastic, organic, e-waste, paper, metal or glass.
02
Dispose Right
Get exact disposal instructions and the nearest bin location on campus. Mark it as disposed to earn eco-points.
03
Earn & Redeem
Collect eco-points from scans and games. Redeem them for canteen discounts, Swiggy vouchers, Amazon gift cards and more.
